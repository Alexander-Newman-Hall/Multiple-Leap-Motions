Qt 6.8.3

The controller and engine dynamically use Qt libraries. The app includes
frameworks, plugins and QML resources from Qt Base, Qt Declarative and Qt SVG.
Qt is copyright The Qt Company and the respective contributors. This build
uses the open-source libraries under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 3. The license texts and Qt exception are under licenses/.

The 2.0.1 release provides the corresponding source archives for all three
Qt modules alongside the packaged app. Their filenames, upstream URLs and
SHA-256 checksums are in qt-sources.json. The source archives include the
modules' third-party code and license notices.
https://github.com/Alexander-Newman-Hall/Multiple-Leap-Motions/releases/tag/v2.0.1

You can rebuild Hand Bridge against compatible modified Qt libraries. The
Mac build instructions use CMAKE_PREFIX_PATH to select your Qt installation,
and the packaging script takes the same installation as its qt_prefix.
The LGPL permits reverse engineering to debug your Qt modifications.
Qt documents its source build at https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.8/build-sources.html

nlohmann/json 3.11.3 by Niels Lohmann and oscpack by Ross Bencina both use

Ultraleap Hyperion

For live tracking, Hand Bridge loads LeapC from Ultraleap's separately
installed Hyperion distribution. Obtain the tracking software and SDK from
Ultraleap under its terms.
https://www.ultraleap.com/downloads/leap-motion-controller-2/
